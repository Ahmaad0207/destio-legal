Destio Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026.

Destio is an app for goals, tasks and focus, with an assistant called Tio. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it is kept, who receives it and how to delete it.

Developer: Ahmad Jalolov, Uzbekistan. Contact: camilajalolova@gmail.com.

In short

  • No sign-up. The app creates an anonymous ID — no name, email or phone.
  • Most of what you write stays on your phone.
  • Tio receives data only after your explicit consent, and only to answer you.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps, no selling of data. AI models are not trained on your data.
  • Everything stored on our server can be deleted with one button in the app.

1. Account

On first launch the app creates an anonymous account on our server (Supabase). It lets the server tell whose data, limits and subscription are whose. We do not collect your name, email, phone number or login details.

2. Kept only on your phone

Tasks and habits, notes, your conversations with Tio, what Tio remembers about you, settings, and the apps you chose for the Screen Time shield. This data stays on the device until you delete the app. Part of it is sent to Tio when you talk to it — see section 4.

3. Stored on the Destio server

  • The anonymous account ID.
  • Goals: title, why it matters to you, how you'll know you reached it, what may get in the way and how to handle it, plan steps and daily tasks.
  • Daily totals: how many tasks you had and how many you completed (for your streak and stats). After AI consent, also the titles of tasks left undone that day, so Tio has context.
  • A service log of Tio conversations — without text: time, model, token count, cost, actions Tio performed, your rating of a reply and, if a safety check triggered, its category. The text of your messages and of Tio's replies is not stored on the server.
  • Subscription and limits: subscription status, App Store country (for regional pricing), message counters, your consents.
  • Tio's spoken replies, if you turned voice on: audio files, so a reply can be replayed.
  • A shared web-search cache: summaries of results for anonymized topics, kept up to 30 days and not linked to you.

4. Tio and third-party AI services

Nothing is sent to the assistant before you consent. After consent, to answer you, Tio sends to AI services:

  • your message and the earlier messages of the conversation;
  • photos you chose to attach;
  • context: task and habit titles, note titles and first lines, and the text of only those notes that match your question when you ask about your notes; goals and their steps, what Tio remembers, your streak; progress journal entries — results you told Tio in words; Tio's suggestions from its previous reply that you have not yet confirmed;
  • for planning, busy hours in your calendar — without event titles.

Recipients:

  • OpenAI — main model, safety checks of messages, web search and speech recognition. We request replies without storage (store: false); under OpenAI's terms, API data is not used for training and is kept up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.
  • Anthropic — backup model when OpenAI is unavailable. Under Anthropic's terms, data is not used for training without permission and is kept up to 30 days (up to 2 years if flagged by their safety systems).
  • ElevenLabs — only if you turned on spoken replies: receives the text of Tio's reply.

Web search. The search service receives only a short anonymized topic. Your name, contact details, task titles and personal details are not sent.

Voice input. Dictation is recognized by Apple (on the device or on Apple's servers), and for accuracy the recording is also transcribed by OpenAI. Hints are sent with it — titles of your goals and notes — so words are recognized correctly. We do not store voice recordings.

You can withdraw consent at any time: Profile → AI access.

5. Device permissions

  • Calendar — to add tasks to a separate "Destio" calendar and see busy hours when planning. Your event titles are not sent.
  • Photos and camera — to attach a picture to a Tio conversation and save a card to your library. Only what you pick is sent.
  • Notifications — reminders are scheduled on the phone and do not go through our server.
  • Screen Time — the shield uses Apple's framework. Only the system knows which apps you chose; neither we nor the app can see their names.

Each permission can be revoked in iOS Settings.

6. Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We receive subscription status through RevenueCat, which gets the anonymous ID and purchase details. We never see payment details.

7. Crash reports

If the app fails, an error report (the error text and where in the code it happened) may be sent to Sentry. Your entries, messages and ID are not added to it.

8. Service alerts

If AI spending on an account is unusually high, the developer gets an alert in Telegram with the anonymous ID and the amount — no content.

9. Deletion and your rights

  • Profile → Data → Delete server data deletes the account and everything linked to it on the server: goals, daily totals, the log, your subscription record in our database, consents, spoken replies. Only AI-cost records with no link to you and the shared search cache remain. This does not cancel your subscription — do that in App Store settings.
  • Forget history with Tio deletes conversations and the service log.
  • Withdrawing AI consent stops data from being sent to AI services and erases task titles from the server.
  • Data on the phone is deleted with the app.
  • Data at OpenAI, Anthropic and ElevenLabs is deleted on their schedules (section 4).

You may ask what data about you we hold, and have it corrected or deleted. Write to camilajalolova@gmail.com — we reply within 30 days.

10. Security

Data is sent only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). AI service keys are kept on the server, not in the app.

11. Children

Destio is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this link with a new date. We will tell you about significant changes in the app.
